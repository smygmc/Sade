# Sade

A customizable minimalist Android launcher that helps you use your phone more intentionally.

## A simpler home screen

- Keep up to five favorite apps on your home screen.
- Find your apps with search, on a second page or in an app drawer.
- Make it yours with custom background and text colors, fonts and text sizes.
- Choose an analog or digital clock, show the date, and add optional camera and phone shortcuts.

## More control over your screen time

- Organize apps into groups with shared rules and a separate daily allowance for each app.
- Set different limits for different days.
- Get reminders during continuous use, with messages you can customize.
- Add a pause before opening an app to give yourself a moment to reconsider.
- Block apps when their daily limit is reached or during selected days and hours.
- See your daily usage progress at a glance.

Screen time controls also work when Sade is not your default launcher.

## Quiet and personal

No ads or analytics. Available in 50 languages, with your system language selected by default when supported.
